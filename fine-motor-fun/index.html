How scoring works

Dot-to-dot: connecting the dots in order, and how straight each line stays between two dots. Mazes: staying inside the path, wall touches, and reaching the finish. Tracing: staying on the line and finishing the picture. Brush coloring: staying inside the lines and how much of the picture is colored.
