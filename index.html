Counter Tools

Everything for the snack counter, in one place. Tap the one you need.

Scan Log Practice The pretend till. Ring up a customer, make change, then print the results. Stock the Shelf The stocking game. Match tags, sort by aisle, check the UPC, restock the shelf. Scan & Find Station Find the item on the shelf and scan it. Rounds, hints and read-aloud. Shelf Check The yes-and-no inventory list, with pictures and a PDF at the end. Scan Log The real till for the counter. Scans, cash and change, and the day's totals.

On the iPad: open the page you want, then tap the share button and choose Add to Home Screen. Each one gets its own icon.

Nothing typed into these pages leaves the device it is used on. Student names and results stay on that iPad until a PDF or spreadsheet is saved.
