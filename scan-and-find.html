Check barcodes against the real packages

Walk the shelf and scan each package. The page tells you straight away whether that code is the one it has stored, and lets you fix it on the spot. Worth doing once for the whole store — a tag printed with a box or case code will not match the single item a student picks up.

Assignment lists

Tick the exact products a student has to find and save the set under a name. Saved lists show up in Round settings next to the product groups, so you can assign "Week 1 — Drinks" instead of picking a category.

Import a product list

Bring in a spreadsheet of products — a CSV or a tab-separated export works. Rows are matched on barcode first, then on name: a match updates that product, anything else is added as a new one. Pictures are not part of the file; add those on the product form below.
