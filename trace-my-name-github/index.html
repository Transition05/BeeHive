Palm protection

What can write

Each time the tool opens, fingers and a stylus both write, with palm protection on: while one finger is tracing, a palm or second finger is ignored so the line never jumps or stops. "Stylus after it's used" stops fingers once a stylus touches the screen. "Stylus only" lets only a stylus write. Both last until the page is closed.

Hand rest area

Adds a space where the hand can rest without drawing. "Beside" goes on the writing-hand side (left when Left-handed is on).

Hold to leave Student view

Student view is the locked mode: students can't open settings, Progress or PDFs. To leave, press and hold "Hold to exit" for this long. There is no code.

How scoring works

Stayed on the path is the share of ink inside the tracing path. Finished the letters is how much of each letter the ink covered. The accuracy score balances the two, so scribbling or tracing only half both score lower. Formation checks that each letter starts at the green dot and that each stroke goes the Zaner-Bloser direction. It does not check stroke order. When formation checks are on, stars use 70% accuracy and 30% formation.
